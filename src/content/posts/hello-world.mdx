This is the first post on this blog. I wanted somewhere quiet to think out loud — short essays, half-formed ideas, the occasional poll for the audience.

## What this is

A single column. Serif type. A muted palette. Nothing else.

The goal is to make writing here feel like opening a notebook, not opening a CMS.

## What's a poll doing in a blog?

Sometimes I'd like to know what readers think before I write the next post. Embedded polls are the lowest-friction way to ask.
